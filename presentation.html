Touchless Optical Biometrics & Guided Self-Exams

Eliminating wearable sensor mass while catching early physiological degradation in deep space.

📷 Webcam rPPG & PERCLOS Monitor

Uses remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) via the webcam to detect microscopic hemoglobin absorption changes in facial skin capillaries, extracting pulse rate touchlessly.

PERCLOS Drowsiness Detection: Tracks eyelid closure percentages and blink frequencies to alert astronauts to impending micro-sleep and fatigue before critical maneuvers.

👁️ Autonomous SANS & Cognitive Suite

Digital Snellen & Amsler Grid: Astronauts self-evaluate hyperopic shift and choroidal folds to detect Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome (SANS) early.

Simulated Ocular Ultrasound & 3-Min PVT: Guided tele-ultrasound probe placement on the eyelid to gauge optic nerve sheath diameter (ONSD), paired with millisecond-precision reaction testing.

MICROFLUIDIC COMPATIBILITY:
Integrated with ASTRO-LAB™ microfluidics simulator for Zero-G acoustic separation and dry-reagent test cartridges (Metabolic, Renal Calcium, Biodosimetry, Salivary Stress Swab).
